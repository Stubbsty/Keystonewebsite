Café + kitchen cleaning

Why it matters

After a full day of service, the last thing your team wants is another hour with a mop. That hour usually gets rushed, and it shows.

Keystone takes over the deep part of the close: degreasing benches and canopies, scrubbing floors and drains, and resetting front of house. We work to a written routine built around how your kitchen actually runs, from a 30-seat café to a full restaurant.

Our founder holds a food safety supervisor qualification, so the routine is built with food safety in mind, not bolted on afterwards.

What's included.

Questions

Good questions.

Do you clean exhaust canopies and ducts?

We degrease accessible canopy surfaces and filters as part of a deep clean. Full duct cleaning is specialist work we can refer you for.

Do we need to be there?

No. Most hospitality clients give us a key or code. We lock up, arm the alarm and log the visit.

Are your products food safe?

Yes. Food-contact surfaces are cleaned and sanitised with food-safe products, agreed with you up front.

Can you do a one-off deep clean first?

Yes. Many kitchens start with a deep clean to reset everything, then move to a regular schedule.
