Warehouse + industrial cleaning

Why it matters

In a warehouse, dust and debris are not just untidy. They are a slip risk, they get into stock and equipment, and they make a site look run-down to the clients who visit.

Keystone cleans warehouses, depots and light industrial sites with floor scrubbers for open areas and a full routine for amenities, lunchrooms and site offices. We work to your site induction and WHS rules, wear the PPE your site requires, and schedule around shift changes and loading windows.

We know these sites from the inside. Our founder's background is in production and logistics operations, where the floor has to be safe and the shift has to run on time.

The scope

What's included.

A starting point. Your written scope is built at the walkthrough. Amenities usually need daily cleaning; open warehouse floors are often scrubbed weekly or fortnightly.

Nightly

Every visit

  • Amenities + toilets cleaned, restocked
  • Lunchroom benches, tables, sink + appliances
  • Site office floors, desks + bins
  • Bins emptied, liners replaced
  • High-touch points: doors, rails, clocks
+ Weekly

Weekly

  • Warehouse floors machine-scrubbed
  • Dock + entry areas swept
  • Locker rooms + showers detail
  • Glass + internal partitions
+ Monthly or quarterly

Monthly or quarterly

  • Racking base + aisle edges
  • High dusting where safely accessible
  • Line-marking areas detail
  • Scope + WHS review with site manager

Questions

Good questions.

Will you complete our site induction?

Yes. Every crew member completes your induction before their first shift on site.

Do you bring a floor scrubber?

Yes, for floor areas that suit one. We confirm floor type and access at the walkthrough.

Can you clean during operating hours?

Amenities and offices, yes. Open floor scrubbing is scheduled around forklift traffic and loading windows.

Do you service the TradeCoast?

Yes. Murarrie, Eagle Farm, Pinkenba, Hemmant and the surrounding industrial areas are core service areas.
