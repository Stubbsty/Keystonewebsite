Office cleaning

Why it matters

Your office is judged at 8:45am by people who never think about cleaning, until something is missed.

Most office cleaning contracts fail quietly. A bin skipped here, a smeared glass wall there, a kitchen that smells by Wednesday. Nobody complains formally; they just stop trusting the clean. Keystone runs offices the way a good operations team runs a shift: a written scope for every area, a checklist the crew works through, and a sign-off you can see.

We clean CBD towers, suburban business parks and single-floor suites across Brisbane. Whether you are 200 square metres or a full floor, you get the same thing: a walkthrough before we quote, a scope you approve, and one person to call.

The scope

What's included.

A starting point. Your written scope is built at the walkthrough. Most offices run 3 or 5 nights a week. Small suites often start at 2 nights plus a monthly detail clean.

Nightly

Every visit

  • Vacuum carpets + mop hard floors
  • Empty bins, replace liners
  • Kitchen benches, sink + appliance fronts
  • Washrooms cleaned, restocked
  • High-touch points: handles, switches, lift buttons
  • Reception + meeting rooms reset
+ Weekly

Weekly

  • Glass partitions + internal doors
  • Desks wiped (clear desks)
  • Fridge wipe-out + expired food check
  • Skirting + spot-clean walls
  • Kitchen appliances inside: microwave
+ Monthly or quarterly

Monthly or quarterly

  • High dusting: vents, ledges, frames
  • Carpet spot treatment
  • Upholstered chairs vacuumed
  • Washroom detail + grout
  • Scope review with your contact

Questions

Good questions.

Do you clean during business hours?

Our default is after hours or early morning so the clean is finished before your team arrives. Day porter cover can be scoped for larger sites.

Can we keep the same cleaner?

Yes. Every site gets an assigned crew. If someone is on leave, the replacement is briefed on your scope before they arrive.

Do you supply products and equipment?

Yes. Consumables like paper and soap can be supplied by us or you; we agree that in the scope.

How do you price office cleaning?

After a free walkthrough. Price depends on floor area, number of washrooms and kitchens, and how many nights a week. You get a fixed monthly price.
