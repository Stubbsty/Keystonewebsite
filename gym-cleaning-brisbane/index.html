Gym + fitness cleaning

Why it matters

Members do not cancel because of one dirty bench. They cancel because of the feeling that builds over a month of sticky handles and a smelly change room.

Keystone cleans gyms in the low-traffic window, usually late night or very early morning, so the floor is clear and equipment is ready for the first session. We follow a set route: equipment, floors, mirrors, then wet areas, every visit.

For 24/7 gyms we schedule around your quietest hours and work alongside members safely.

The scope

What's included.

A starting point. Your written scope is built at the walkthrough. Most gyms need a nightly clean, with wet areas sometimes twice a day.

Nightly

Every visit

  • Equipment, benches + handles wiped down
  • Rubber floors vacuumed + mopped
  • Mirrors + glass streak-free
  • Change rooms, showers + toilets
  • Reception + water stations
  • Bins, towels bins + liners
+ Weekly

Weekly

  • Under + behind machines
  • Shower grout + drains
  • Lockers inside + out
  • Studio floors detail
+ Monthly

Monthly

  • High dusting, fans + vents
  • Rubber floor deep scrub
  • Upholstery on benches + pads
  • Route review with gym manager

Questions

Good questions.

Can you clean while the gym is open?

Yes, for 24/7 gyms. We clean in zones during the quietest hours and keep signage out on wet floors.

What do you use on equipment?

Gym-safe disinfectants that are effective and safe for upholstery, grips and screens.

Do you clean showers and change rooms daily?

Yes, every visit. Grout and drains get a weekly detail.

Do you supply towels or consumables?

We can supply soap and paper. Towel services are separate.
