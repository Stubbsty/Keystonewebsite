The process

Six steps. Every site.

  1. 01

    Site walkthrough

    We visit, look at every area and ask how your space is used. 20 to 40 minutes.

  2. 02

    Written scope + fixed price

    Every area, task and frequency in writing, with a fixed monthly price.

  3. 03

    Keys, alarms + access

    Keys and fobs signed into a register. Alarm codes and site rules documented.

  4. 04

    Crew briefing

    Your assigned crew is walked through the site and the scope before their first clean.

  5. 05

    Every visit, signed off

    The crew works through the checklist and logs time on site and completion.

  6. 06

    Review + adjust

    Regular check-ins with your contact. The scope changes when your site does.

Security + access

Your keys, handled properly.

Key register

Every key and fob signed in and out. You always know who holds what.

Alarms + lock-up

Alarm armed and doors checked on exit, every visit, logged.

Site rules

Clear desks, restricted rooms, parking, inductions: written into the scope.

Entry + exit times

Logged each visit, so you can see when we were on site.

Proof, not promises

Signed off. Every visit.

Each visit ends with the checklist completed and logged. Washrooms and kitchens can carry a visible clean-check sheet so your staff can see it too, with a QR code to report anything we missed.

Questions

Good questions.

How long does the walkthrough take?

Usually 20 to 40 minutes depending on the size of the site.

How soon can you start?

Once the scope is approved, we confirm keys and access and set a start date with you.

What happens if something is missed?

Tell your contact. We fix it, and if it is a scope gap we update the scope.
